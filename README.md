## Academic Projects

### 01. Academic Procrastination and Associated Worry
**Research area:** Quantitative Psychology

A collaborative research project examining academic procrastination and worry among university students, including differences across gender and age groups.

- Designed and conducted an online questionnaire study
- Applied established psychological measurement scales
- Analysed results using R, including independent-samples t-tests and two-way ANOVA
- Interpreted findings and discussed methodological limitations

[Project Repository](https://github.com/yejinjung527/academic-procrastination-research)

### 02. Looking Beyond the Grades: A Psychological Assessment of Academic Decline
**Research area:** Clinical Psychology and Psychological Assessment

A simulated clinical case assessment exploring potential psychological explanations for declining academic performance.

- Developed a hypothesis-driven assessment approach
- Examined competing diagnostic explanations
- Considered interviews, collateral information, behavioural observations, and standardised measures
- Developed an integrated diagnostic formulation and recommendations

*This is an academic simulated case, not a real clinical assessment or diagnosis.*

[Project Repository](https://github.com/yejinjung527/psychological-diagnostic-assessment)

### 04. From Cradle to Connection: Attachment Theory as a Developmental Revolution
**Research area:** Developmental Psychology

An individual academic paper exploring the historical development and contemporary relevance of attachment theory.

- Examined the contributions of John Bowlby and Mary Ainsworth
- Reviewed the core principles of attachment theory
- Discussed empirical findings across the lifespan
- Explored applications in relationships, education, and mental health

[Project Repository](https://github.com/yejinjung527/attachment-theory)

### 05. Qualitative Observation and Critical Reflection
**Research area:** Qualitative and Interdisciplinary Approaches

An academic project combining field observations, critical engagement with theoretical readings, and personal reflection.

- Conducted structured observations and developed fieldnotes
- Engaged critically with academic literature
- Explored positionality and reflexivity
- Examined relationships between humans and the natural environment

[Project Repository](https://github.com/yejinjung527/qualitative-observation)
